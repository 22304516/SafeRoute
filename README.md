# 🛡️ SafeRoute — Solo Travel Protection Shield

SafeRoute is a personal safety mobile application designed to protect solo travelers, commuters, and fitness enthusiasts. By transforming your smartphone into an active safety monitor, the app uses your phone's built-in motion sensors and GPS location tracking to detect sudden accidents (like severe falls or collisions) and automatically coordinates emergency alerts when you are unable to call for help yourself.

Built with an **Offline-First** philosophy, SafeRoute keeps your data processing and safety loops running directly on your device, ensuring it works seamlessly even when traveling through dead zones with zero cellular reception.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Journey Guard Dashboard:** A central control deck to activate live trip tracking, monitor your current movement stats, or immediately trigger a manual emergency SOS broadcast with a single tap.
* **Fall Detection Radar:** Uses your phone's internal motion sensors to instantly flag sudden, high-impact accidents. If an impact is detected, it triggers an immediate 10-second warning window allowing you to dismiss false alarms before help is dispatched.
* **Live Map Visualizer:** Displays your live location on an interactive map, automatically drawing a visual breadcrumb trail of where you have traveled so your path can easily be referenced.
* **Guardian Ledger:** A dedicated contact manager where you can save, update, and manage your primary emergency contacts entirely within the app’s secure storage memory.
* **Automated Safety Lifeline:** If the fall radar detects a major incident and you fail to cancel the 10-second countdown, the app bypasses standard menus to instantly transmit your live location data directly to your saved Guardians.
